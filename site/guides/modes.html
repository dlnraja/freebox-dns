Smart spit — 4 modes

La VM héberge son DNS : listes locales d’abord, puis forward Unbound (DoT uncensoring).

flowchart LR
  Q[Client] --> H[Hosts locaux anti-lie]
  H --> M{Mode}
  M -->|uncensored| U[Unbound]
  M -->|malware| B1[Blocky malware]
  M -->|antipub| B2[Blocky ads+uBO]
  M -->|secure| B3[Blocky full]
  B1 --> U
  B2 --> U
  B3 --> U
  U --> W[DoT / root]
ModePort DNSDoHEffet
uncensored:53:8453Aucune pub bloquée — anti-censure
malware:5357:8445Menaces seulement
antipub:5358:8446Pubs / trackers / anti-adblock
secure:5354:8444antipub + malware
DHCP : DNS1 = 9.9.9.10 (SOS Quad9), DNS2 = IP VM (uncensored). Les autres modes = DoH / port manuel.
